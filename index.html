Privacy Policy for Snow Hop

Effective date: October 17, 2026
Developer: 1Muhammad
Contact: wafashakeel58@gmail.com

Snow Hop ("the game") is a climbing game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. It is written to be read, so it is short.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, and you are never asked for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your scores, settings and progress stay on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To do that, Google's ad software collects some device information, described below.
  • The game is for people aged 13 and over.

What stays on your device

The game saves your best scores, settings, Frost Shards, owned characters and challenge progress on your phone. We do not receive this information and cannot see it. Uninstalling the game removes it. You can also clear it from the game's Settings screen ("Reset progress").

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to show two kinds of ads: rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example, to continue a run), and ads shown between runs.

To serve and measure ads, Google's software on your device may collect:

  • your advertising ID (a resettable identifier that Android gives to apps),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • information about how ads were shown and whether they were interacted with.

We do not receive this information. It goes to Google, which uses it under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads that use personal data are shown. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Play without ads. Ads need an internet connection. The game works offline and simply shows no ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will help.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not ask for your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. The game requests only network access (to load ads), the ability to vibrate, and access to the advertising ID.

Security and retention

We keep nothing on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Ad data held by Google is subject to Google's retention practices.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
