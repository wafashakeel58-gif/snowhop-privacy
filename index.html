Privacy Policy for Snow Hop

Effective date: October 25, 2026
Developer: 1Muhammad
Contact: wafashakeel58@gmail.com

Snow Hop ("the game") is a climbing game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. It is written to be read, so it is short.

The short version

  • You are never asked for your name, email or phone number. Signing in with Google Play Games is optional.
  • Your progress stays on your device, unless you sign in with Google Play Games, which saves it to your Google account.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To do that, Google's ad software collects some device information, described below.
  • Optional in-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • The game is for people aged 16 and over.

What stays on your device

The game saves your best scores, settings, Frost Shards, owned characters and challenge progress on your phone. We do not receive this information and cannot see it. Uninstalling the game removes it. You can also clear it from the game's Settings screen ("Reset progress").

Google Play Games (optional)

You can sign in with your Google account through Google Play Games. If you do:

  • Your progress is saved to your Google account, so it follows you to a new phone. This is your best scores, stats, Frost Shards, boosters, characters and challenge progress. It is stored by Google in your Play Games saved games, not on our servers.
  • Your best score is sent to the world leaderboard, where other players can see it next to your Play Games name and profile picture. Only scores from runs that did not use a booster or a continue are sent.
  • Google tells the game your Play Games player ID and display name, so it can show you your own row on the leaderboard.

This is handled by Google under its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can play without signing in; your progress then stays on your device only. To remove your saved game, use the Play Games app or your Google account's data settings.

In-app purchases

You can buy packs of Frost Shards. Purchases are made through Google Play, which handles your payment details under its own terms and privacy policy. We never receive your card or payment information. Google Play tells the game only which pack was bought and gives it a purchase reference, so the shards can be added to your game exactly once. That reference is kept on your device.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to show two kinds of ads: rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example, to continue a run), and ads shown between runs. Through AdMob mediation, ads may also come from AppLovin and Unity Ads, which bid to fill an ad space; AdMob picks the winning ad.

To serve and measure ads, Google's software on your device may collect:

  • your advertising ID (a resettable identifier that Android gives to apps),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • information about how ads were shown and whether they were interacted with.

We do not receive this information. It goes to Google and, when one of them shows an ad, to AppLovin or Unity. Each uses it under its own privacy policy: Google policies.google.com/privacy, AppLovin applovin.com/privacy, Unity unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads that use personal data are shown. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Play without ads. Ads need an internet connection. The game works offline and simply shows no ads.

More Games screen

The More Games screen downloads a short list of our other games from our website, which is hosted by GitHub. This is an ordinary request for a public file; the game sends no identifiers with it. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address when the file is requested: GitHub privacy statement.

Children

The game is not directed at children and is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will help.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not ask for your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. The game requests only network access (to load ads), the ability to vibrate, access to the advertising ID, and Google Play billing (for optional purchases).

Security and retention

We keep nothing on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Ad data held by Google is subject to Google's retention practices.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
